# Reasoning Quality Rubric v0.1

Score each dimension from 0 to 4.

## 1. Relevant fact use

- 0 — no registered relevant facts;
- 1 — one weak or vague fact;
- 2 — multiple relevant facts with important omissions or errors;
- 3 — several relevant facts used accurately;
- 4 — accurate use of the most decision-relevant facts.

## 2. Counterevidence integration

- 0 — contrary evidence ignored or denied;
- 1 — mentioned dismissively;
- 2 — acknowledged but not integrated;
- 3 — meaningfully affects the reasoning;
- 4 — explicitly weighed against supporting evidence.

## 3. Opposing argument reconstruction

- 0 — absent;
- 1 — caricature or weak objection;
- 2 — partially accurate;
- 3 — strong and accurate;
- 4 — a reasonable opponent would accept the reconstruction.

## 4. Revision conditions

- 0 — no possible revision condition;
- 1 — vague or impossible;
- 2 — relevant but poorly specified;
- 3 — concrete and decision-relevant;
- 4 — concrete, measurable, and proportionate.

Maximum score: 16.

Evaluators must ignore polish, vocabulary, length, and rhetorical confidence except where these directly affect the four registered dimensions.
