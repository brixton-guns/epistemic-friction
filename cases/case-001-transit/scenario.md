# Case 001 — The Valdora Transit Decision

Valdora is a fictional city of 450,000 residents. Its council has funding for one major transit programme and must choose between two options.

## Option A — Electric Bus Network

Replace part of the existing fleet with battery-electric buses, add charging depots, and increase service frequency on several routes.

## Option B — Light Rail Line

Build one high-capacity light rail corridor connecting the northern residential districts, the city centre, and the southern employment zone.

Both proposals are feasible within the fictional setting. Neither is intended to be objectively correct.

## Participant task

Choose Option A or Option B using only the registered information supplied during the study.

Before and after the intervention, report:

1. your choice;
2. confidence from 0 to 100;
3. your reasons;
4. the strongest argument against your choice;
5. the evidence that would make you reconsider.

All places, costs, forecasts, and institutions in this case are fictional.
